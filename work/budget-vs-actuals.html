Did federal departments spend what they were given?

Every year Parliament approves a budget for each federal department, adds more during the year, and the Public Accounts report what was actually spent. I compared the three for about 140 departments over 16 years to see how reliable the original budget is, and where money goes unspent.

Data: Public Accounts of Canada, PSPC open data (real)Scope: 2009/10 to 2024/25, 60 ministriesTools: Power BI, DAX, Python

    03 · WHERE

    A handful of departments leave the most unspent, year after year

    Ten departments account for about three-quarters of the money left unspent since 2020/21. Most are recurring cases, over 10% unspent in at least three of the last five years, which points to program design rather than one bad year.

    Treasury Board's central votes are different: they are set aside for government-wide needs and lapse by design, so they are labelled rather than flagged.

    Recommendations

    Budget office

    Track against the final budget, not just the original

    Report variance to both, so mid-year funding is visible instead of hiding inside a single variance number.

    Measure: spending vs original and vs final budget
    Department finance leads

    Treat recurring underspending as a planning problem

    Where a program lapses over 10% three years running, re-profile funding to the years it will actually be spent.

    Measure: years over 10% unspent, last five
    Analysts

    Label structural lapses before flagging

    Central funds and contingencies lapse by design; separating them keeps the attention list credible.

    Measure: unspent excluding central funds

    Data, model and method

    01 · SOURCEPublic AccountsAuthorities and spending by department, 2009/10 to 2024/25
    02 · CHECKPythonBudgetary rows only; verified available = spent + carried + lapsed every year
    03 · MODELStar schemaBudget fact with year, ministry and department dimensions
    04 · MEASUREDAXFinal budget, unspent %, 3-year average, recurring pattern, budget walk
    05 · SECURERow-level securityMinistry manager role filters to the signed-in user's ministries

    Core measures (from the model)

    Final Budget =
[Original Budget] + [Added Mid-year] + [Transfers and Adjustments] + [Carried In]

Unspent % 3-yr Avg =
VAR Y = MAX ( 'Fiscal Year'[Year End] )
VAR Win =
    CALCULATETABLE ( VALUES ( 'Fiscal Year'[Year End] ), REMOVEFILTERS ( 'Fiscal Year' ), 'Fiscal Year'[Year End] > Y - 3, 'Fiscal Year'[Year End] <= Y )
RETURN
    DIVIDE ( CALCULATE ( [Unspent], REMOVEFILTERS ( 'Fiscal Year' ), Win ), CALCULATE ( [Final Budget], REMOVEFILTERS ( 'Fiscal Year' ), Win ) )

Pattern =
SWITCH (
    TRUE (),
    [Is Central Fund], "Central fund",
    [Unspent %] <= 0.10, BLANK (),
    [Years Over 10% Unspent] >= 3, "Recurring",
    "Occasional"
)

    Limits of this analysis

    • Annual figures only; the Public Accounts don't publish monthly spending, so there is no in-year forecast.
    • Department names and ministry portfolios change over time; departments are tracked by their reported name.
    • Unspent funds are not waste: some are re-profiled to later years or held as contingencies.
    • Status thresholds (5% and 10%) are my own, for comparison only.
