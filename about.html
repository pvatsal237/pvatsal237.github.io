About

Vatsal Patel

I work in Business Intelligence and Analytics in Toronto. I build the reporting that program, finance and operations leaders use to make monthly decisions, from the SQL Server and SSIS pipelines underneath to the Power BI models and pages on top.

At the Ontario Ministry of Children, Community and Social Services I build dashboards on Ontario Works and ODSP caseloads, spend and intake, and executive briefings on caseload forecasts and budget variance. I moved more than 40 legacy SSRS reports to Power BI, and I set up AI-assisted Power BI development with Power BI project files and Claude Code, which cut model and report build time for our developers.

Before that, at Spadeworks, I worked with hospital and banking clients: emergency-department length-of-stay reporting against provincial targets, and a Power BI solution that tracked a green auto-lending target using loan, vehicle-registration and subsidy data. I started in IT operations with the Ontario Public Service, which is why I care about access, security and things that keep working after hand-off.

About this portfolio

Most of my work over the last four years has been on confidential government and client data, so I can't show it. Instead I rebuilt the kinds of analyses I do using public and synthetic data. Each case study says exactly which data is real and which is synthetic, and credits the original source. One project, Donor Campaign Effectiveness, also shows the AI-assisted workflow I use at work. The analysis choices and the conclusions are mine throughout, and I'm happy to walk through any of them.
